About Us

From a club hobbyist to a show-day toolkit

ShowLeaders started with a hobbyist in the Rocky Mountain Koi Club in Colorado who saw a chance to use his technical skills to run the club’s auctions and koi show.

As the software grew more capable, it got noticed and began supporting other shows. Online pieces were added, the art of the possible started turning creative gears, and now we’re here.

ShowLeaders is owned by Chris Goumas.
